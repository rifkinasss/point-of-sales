# Anti-slop audit 002

Scope: approved Livewire Cashier POS workspace findings 1–6.
Mode: AFTER.
Date: 2026-10-04.

## Findings approved and addressed

1. **HIGH — Brand token classes were still expressed as indigo utilities.** Addressed by using NasLabs `primary`, `primary-soft`, `primary-hover`, `border`, `secondary`, and `muted` tokens in the POS workspace and product card source.

2. **MEDIUM — Desktop workstation proportions were not explicit.** Addressed with a responsive product-first grid: intermediate two-column layout at `lg`, and approximately 65/35 product/cart tracks at `xl` with a deliberate gap.

3. **MEDIUM — The product grid was not touch-first enough.** Addressed with three product columns at desktop and four at `xl`, larger card hit areas, compact information, and no small button as the primary interaction.

4. **MEDIUM — Cart hierarchy exposed secondary controls inline.** Addressed by presenting a compact `Pelanggan` row and grouping discount/promotion controls behind functional disclosures. Existing Livewire actions remain unchanged.

5. **MEDIUM — Internal scrolling was not coordinated with the workstation viewport.** Addressed with viewport-based desktop heights for the catalog and cart item list while preserving natural stacking below desktop breakpoints.

6. **LOW — Search copy and affordance did not match the cashier brief.** Addressed with a search icon and the placeholder `Cari produk, SKU, atau scan barcode`.
