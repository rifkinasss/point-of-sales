# Anti-Slop Audit 001

Scope: Phase 5 cashier UI changes and adjacent existing POS presentation.
Mode: AFTER.
Date: 2026-10-04.

## Findings

1. **Low — Indigo-to-violet gradients remain in the cashier shell and primary payment action.**
   The gradient gives the cashier surface a clear active-workstation identity and is limited to the shell/cart/payment affordance, so it is not a hard-gate failure. It is still close to the generic blue-purple treatment and should be reconsidered if the next visual pass aims for a flatter operational palette.

2. **Low — The POS uses several rounded surfaces and soft shadows at once.**
   Product cards, the cart, search field, and workstation header use radius/shadow to separate touch targets and establish hierarchy. The combination is readable and functional, but a future polish pass could reduce one layer of shadow without affecting task completion.

3. **Low — Tablet checkout keeps the cart in the same two-column workspace rather than offering a dedicated cart drawer.**
   The current breakpoint keeps product browsing and cart review visible together at supported tablet widths. A drawer could provide more product room, but introducing it would add interaction state and is outside this Phase 5 baseline.

## Gate result

No hard-gate accessibility or functional defect was observed in the source review. Contrast checks passed for the primary indigo, success green, and warning amber text colors against white. No changes were made after this audit, per AFTER mode.
